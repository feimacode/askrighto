# Worked example

The request below is invented, and no figures are shown on purpose.

User: "Owner-occupier, variable, principal and interest, 80% LVR. What have two lenders published?"

Good answer shape: say you read the product lists from the two lenders' own Consumer Data Right endpoints just now (give the time), that Ask Righto holds no lender data, and which lenders you covered. For each product give the lender, product name, published rate, the lender's own comparison rate with "for the lender's example loan", the link to the lender's page, and any fee shown separately. Say nothing about which is better. Offer repayment arithmetic on a rate with `calculate_home_loan_repayments`.
