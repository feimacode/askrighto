# Reading published rates

- **Rate and comparison rate** arrive as fractions: 0.0599 is 5.99%. A rate above 1 is not understood: do not guess, link the lender.
- **Rate type**: VARIABLE, FIXED (with a term such as P2Y), INTRODUCTORY, and DISCOUNT (a margin taken off another rate, not a rate itself).
- **Tiers** limit a rate to an LVR band or a loan size. Use the tier covering the user's own LVR.
- **Comparison rate** is the lender's figure for a stated example loan. It is not a measure of which loan is cheaper.
- **Negotiated or tailored** products publish a starting rate, not a quote.
- **Fees** have a type and a period; show them beside a repayment, never inside it.
- A lender can publish a minimum and a maximum advertised rate for one product and band. Show both.
