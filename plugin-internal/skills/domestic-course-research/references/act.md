# ACT: ACT Senior Secondary Certificate and UAC

Unconfirmed structure facts (see README). Read the official page for anything specific.

- **Certificate:** the ACT Senior Secondary Certificate, run by the ACT Board of Senior Secondary Studies. The Board's pages say which courses count towards a tertiary entrance rank.
- **Admissions centre:** applications go through UAC, the same centre as NSW, but the certificate, its courses and how courses count are the ACT's own, not the HSC's. The ACT Board reports the ATAR (on the Tertiary Entrance Statement it produces), so do not say UAC calculates it.
- **Figures you may meet:** the ATAR and a university's selection rank. Read the label and the intake year.
- **Prerequisites:** written by the university in its own words. Map them to ACT course names only by reading the Board's course list; do not assume an HSC course has the same name or counts the same way.
- **Adjustments:** UAC and the universities list schemes; names and rules change. Read the page.
- **Traps:** do not use NSW pages for ACT rules without saying so. A university in the ACT can list courses through UAC for students of any state.
