# Victoria: VCE and VTAC

Unconfirmed structure facts (see README). Read the official page for anything specific.

- **Certificate:** the Victorian Certificate of Education (VCE), run by the VCAA. Studies are made of units; the later two (units 3 and 4) are the ones universities usually name in prerequisites.
- **Admissions centre:** VTAC takes applications, runs preferences and offers, and calculates the ATAR.
- **Figures you may meet:** the aggregate (a total from scaled study scores), the ATAR (a rank), and a university's published selection rank or lowest selection rank. Universities label these differently; read the label and the intake year.
- **Prerequisites:** usually written as a named study (and sometimes a minimum study score) in units 3 and 4. English or EAL is often named separately.
- **Adjustments:** VTAC lists equity schemes that let universities take a student's circumstances into account; any change to a selection rank is the university's own adjustment factors. Names and rules change; read the VTAC page and the university's page.
- **Traps:** a study can have a similar name at different levels; the page may name the exact study. An aggregate is not an ATAR.
