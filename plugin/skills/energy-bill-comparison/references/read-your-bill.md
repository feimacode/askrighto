# Reading a power bill

General guidance on where to look; bills differ by retailer, so ask the user to read the figures out rather than assuming a layout.

- **Billing period:** two dates near the top, or "days in this bill". The number of days is needed with the usage.
- **Usage:** kilowatt hours (kWh) for electricity, megajoules (MJ) for gas. Look for a usage table or "total usage".
- **Time-of-use split:** if the bill lists peak, shoulder and off-peak usage separately, ask for each figure.
- **Controlled load:** a separate line, often for a hot water circuit.
- **Solar:** exported energy (kWh) and the feed-in rate, if the user has solar.
- **Distributor:** the network name often appears near the supply address or in the charges breakdown.

If the user cannot find a figure, do not fill it in. Say which figure is missing and what the comparison needs it for.
