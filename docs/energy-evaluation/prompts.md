# Energy prompt set (version 1)

Twenty questions on the energy tools (`search_energy_plans`, `get_energy_plan`, `compare_energy_plans`, `summarise_interval_readings`), run by `npm run evals -- --set energy` against the synthetic plan fixtures and benchmark fixture (see `docs/evals.md`) and by hand in each host (`docs/energy-readings-host-test.md` covers the readings route). Each row names the tools expected and what a grounded answer must contain. Prompts are ours; they are not real user questions. Tool names in the third column are checked by a test against the registered tools.

The fixtures hold: NSW 2000 (Ausgrid): one electricity plan with a solar feed-in that varies by time, id ending `@EME`. Victoria 3000 and 3001 (CitiPower): two electricity plans, ids ending `@VEC`. Victoria 3000 (Jemena): two time-of-use plans, one whose windows can be matched to half-hourly readings and one that cannot. Victoria 3000 (AusNet): one gas plan. The benchmark fixture covers postcodes 2000 and 3000 with invented numbers; no WA or NT postcode. In prompts, `{{NEM12:3days}}`, `{{NEM12:2nmi}}` and `{{NEM13}}` stand for small synthetic files (invented NMI and serial) the runner pastes in.

| E01 | What electricity plans can I look at in postcode 2000? | search_energy_plans | list the plans with retailer, plan name and id; give an Energy Made Easy rate sheet link labelled as that; never show a plan id as a link; include the notice |
