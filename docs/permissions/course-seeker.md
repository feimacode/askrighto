# Draft: request to Course Seeker (Department of Education)

Status: DRAFT for Iven to review and send. Contact found 2026-11-13: CourseSeeker@education.gov.au.

**Subject:** Request to use Course Seeker course data in a free information service, and a question about open data

Hello,

I am building Ask Righto (askrighto.com.au), a free, read-only service that helps Australian students and parents ask questions about university courses through AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini. It does not store anything about its users.

We would like to ask two things.

1. **Open data.** Is there a plan to publish Course Seeker's course data (course names, institutions, campuses, entry requirements, ATAR and selection information, links) as open data, for example on data.gov.au under a Creative Commons licence? If so, when, and in what format?
2. **Interim permission.** Until then, may we reuse the following Course Seeker fields for undergraduate courses, with attribution and a link to the course's Course Seeker page: course name, institution, campus, duration, prerequisites, and the latest published entry figures with their kind and year?

How we would use it: figures shown with their kind, year and source link; refreshed at least every year; a standard notice that past figures are not cut-offs or predictions; a correction and removal address with a five business day response. We will not show the data as our own and will remove anything you ask us to.

Kind regards,
Iven Xu, Ask Righto
